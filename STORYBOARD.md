# Online Food Delivery System - Storyboard

## Scene 1 – Customer Login
The customer logs into the online food delivery application.

## Scene 2 – Browse Restaurants
The customer views available restaurants and their menus.

## Scene 3 – Select Food
The customer selects food items and adds them to the cart.

## Scene 4 – Place Order
The customer confirms the food order and delivery address.

## Scene 5 – Payment
The customer selects a payment method and completes the payment.

## Scene 6 – Restaurant
The restaurant receives and prepares the order.

## Scene 7 – Delivery
A delivery partner collects the order and delivers it to the customer.

## Scene 8 – Order Completed
The customer receives the food and can provide a rating or review.
